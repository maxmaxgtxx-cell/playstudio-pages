Privacy & Terms

Play Studio (Verdantlog Play Studio, a trade name of MASTOURI LLC) · 22 Lawn Ave, Albany, NY 12204 · hello@getplaystudio.com · Last updated November 2, 2026

In one sentence: we use your information only to make and deliver what you ordered, we never sell or share it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, organisation, email, phone, delivery address.
  • Order content: the names, logos, messages, photos or voice recordings you ask us to put on a gift or a private page.
  • Payment is handled by Stripe or Shopify Payments. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic technical logs needed to keep pages secure (for example, failed PIN attempts).

How we use it

  • To prepare previews and quotes, produce your order, ship it and answer your questions.
  • We send marketing emails only to businesses, only about our services, and every email has a one-line opt-out that we honour immediately.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Who else handles it (only to fulfil your order)

  • Print and shipping partners (for example Printify print providers) receive only what must be printed and the delivery address.
  • Payment processors (Stripe, Shopify Payments) and our hosting / storage providers, under their own security and privacy terms.
  • Our designs are AI-assisted; every file is reviewed by a person before delivery.

Lists of names (staff, residents, members)

  • We ask only for what will be printed (usually a first name). Please do not send health, room or other sensitive details.
  • Name lists are kept private, used only for that order and deleted within 30 days after delivery.

“Inside / ما بالداخل” private pages

  • Each page has a long random link that is not listed in search engines and is protected by a PIN that the sender shares.
  • Messages, photos and voice files are kept in private storage and are delivered only after the correct PIN is checked on our server. Repeated wrong PINs temporarily lock the page.
  • The sender can edit or delete the page at any time. Pages stay active for 12 months unless the sender renews them; after that we delete their content.
  • Senders must be 18 or older. A recipient's voice reply is recorded only if the recipient chooses to record it.
  • Please only upload photos and recordings you have the right to share.

Security

  • All pages use encrypted connections (HTTPS); stored files are encrypted at rest by our storage provider.
  • Access to customer files is limited to the systems that fulfil orders.

Your choices

  • Email hello@getplaystudio.com to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop emails. We reply within 7 days.

Orders, refunds and production

  • Nothing is produced before you approve the preview and pay.
  • Personalised items are made for you, so they can't be returned for a change of mind; if an item arrives damaged or wrong, we reprint or refund it.
  • Holiday delivery dates depend on ordering by the date shown on the quote.
